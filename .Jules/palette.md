## 2024-05-14 - Add ARIA Labels and Focus Styles to Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Found some icon-only buttons in HeroSection that are missing both `aria-label` attributes and keyboard focus styles (`focus-visible`). This violates WCAG accessibility guidelines.
**Action:** Always verify icon-only buttons have descriptive `aria-label` attributes and `focus-visible` outline styles for keyboard navigation.
