## 2024-05-24 - FloatingAssistant Accessibility
**Learning:** Found several icon-only buttons (close, mic, send) in the FloatingAssistant and a theme toggle in the StickyHeader lacking proper aria-labels and keyboard focus indicators (focus-visible). Specifically, when implementing focus rings in dark themes or contrasting colors, it's essential to apply `focus-visible` rather than just `focus` so that non-keyboard interactions don't trigger the outline, and the color needs to contrast with both the component background and overall app background. I also noticed that the `StickyHeader`'s toggleTheme wasn't actually tied to the button's onClick handler.
**Action:** Always verify icon-only buttons in floating elements or complex header components have `aria-label` for screen readers and `focus-visible:ring-2` with appropriate offset for keyboard navigation. Always make sure interactive buttons have their click handlers properly hooked up.
